Service description

Cloud Music is an audio player for iOS that lets you connect third-party cloud storage services (Google Drive, OneDrive, MEGA, Dropbox, Box, Yandex Disk, etc.), a home NAS (Emby, Jellyfin, Plex), a personal server (FTP, WebDAV, NFS), or local files, to listen to music files you already own. For MEGA specifically, you may either sign in with your MEGA account (email and password) to browse and pick files from your entire account, or connect a single public MEGA share link without signing in at all. Cloud Music does not host, sell or provide any music content: you must have your own audio files and the necessary rights to listen to them.
