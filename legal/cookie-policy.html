This site does not use tracking cookies

This site is a simple static showcase, hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not set any advertising cookies, any audience-measurement cookies, and does not include any third-party tracking tool (no Google Analytics, no advertising pixel, no embedded social network).

Technical cookies related to hosting

Like any site hosted on GitHub Pages, strictly technical cookies may be set by GitHub's infrastructure to ensure the service works properly (for example, load balancing). These cookies are managed by GitHub, Inc., independently of this site's publisher. To learn more, see GitHub's privacy statement.

What about the Cloud Music app?

The Cloud Music iOS app does not use cookies: that mechanism is specific to web browsers. Your preferences, library and listening stats are stored locally on your device (app storage and iOS Keychain), as explained in the privacy policy.

Managing your browser's cookies

You can configure your browser at any time to block or delete cookies set by the sites you visit, including the purely technical ones described above.
