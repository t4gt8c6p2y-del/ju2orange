Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Legal NoticePrivacyCookiesTerms & ConditionsLicense

Cloud Music is a personal music player application for iOS. It lets you connect to cloud storage services (Google Drive, OneDrive, MEGA, Dropbox, Box, Yandex Disk, etc.), a home NAS (Emby, Jellyfin, Plex), or a personal server (FTP, WebDAV, NFS), to listen to or download audio files you already own.

What data is used

Cloud Music only accesses the files and folders in your account or on your device, with your explicit authorization: secure sign-in (OAuth) for cloud services like Google Drive or OneDrive, an encrypted share link for MEGA, or login credentials for a NAS or personal server. The application only requests the permissions strictly necessary to list, read and download audio files.

Where your data is stored

Your login credentials are never transmitted to Cloud Music or to any third-party server: the connection happens directly between your device and the relevant cloud service. Access tokens obtained after signing in are stored only on your device, in iOS's secure Keychain, and never leave it.

Sharing with third parties

Cloud Music does not share, sell or transmit any personal data to third parties. No ads, no trackers, no data collection for commercial purposes.

Subscription payments

Some features may require a paid subscription. Subscriptions are billed and managed entirely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit: Cloud Music never receives or stores your payment details (card number, billing address, etc.). Managing, cancelling or requesting a refund for a subscription is done through your Apple ID account settings.

Deleting your data

You can disconnect from a cloud service or delete all of the application's data at any time from Cloud Music's internal settings. You can also revoke Cloud Music's access directly from the security settings of your Google, Microsoft, MEGA, etc. account.
